## Storage Boundary

| Storage class | Content | Required controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `DB-STRUCTURED` | Canonical financial records, evidence metadata/references, approvals, assignments, policy resolutions, report metadata, audit events, export-attempt metadata | PostgreSQL through Prisma; organization-scoped relations; restrictive deletion; immutable revisions where required; encryption at rest/in transit; backup under P0-08 |
| `OBJECT-PRIVATE` | Uploaded scans, images, PDFs, signed forms, generated report/export artifacts when the approved category permits storage | Private encrypted object storage; no public bucket or permanent public URL; opaque object key; SHA-256 integrity hash, original filename, declared and detected/validated media type, exact byte size, uploader, School/organization scope, evidence category/record, upload time, and source-document date or `NOT AVAILABLE` with reason; access only through short-lived authorized retrieval. The initial pilot has no malware scanning or quarantine subsystem. |
| `EXTERNAL-CONTROLLED` | Physical originals, bank statements, cash-count/custody proof, government-system records, or other artifacts the Blueprint keeps outside the application | Store a structured reference only: issuing/custodian organization, document type/number, source date, physical or system location, responsible custodian, verification status/time/actor, integrity hash when a digital copy is available, and access instructions that contain no secret |
| `SOURCE-REPOSITORY` | Supplied historical policy/research sources already preserved in `reseach/` | Read-only historical source; preserve original bytes and recorded hash; do not modify through P0-07 |

Evidence content must never be embedded in object keys, URLs, correlation IDs, display codes, notification text, or System Logs. Under `PO-REP-2026-08-12-D02`, ordinary evidence upload permits only PDF (`application/pdf`), JPEG (`image/jpeg`), and PNG (`image/png`), with declared-type and actual file signature/content validation where technically feasible. Each artifact is limited to exactly `10 MiB` (`10 * 1024 * 1024` bytes). An evidence record may contain multiple artifacts, but each artifact independently satisfies type, size, integrity, authorization, and scope controls and remains linked to the evidence record. Under `PO-REP-2026-08-12-D03`, SHA-256 and the listed integrity metadata are required, but no malware product, scanner, quarantine state/storage/workflow, scan outcome, release authority, or malware-based acceptance/rejection exists in the initial pilot. The application must not claim an artifact is malware-clean or fabricate scan metadata. P0-08-D01 selects Prisma Postgres Free tier in Singapore only for `DB-STRUCTURED` synthetic/anonymized testing. No object-storage service, region, key owner, operator, encryption, recovery, production suitability, or provider capability is inferred, and `OBJECT-PRIVATE` upload remains disabled.

Under `PO-REP-2026-08-12-D04`, an evidence-access link expires 15 minutes after issuance and a generated export-download link expires 24 hours after generation. A link may be reused before expiry, but every retrieval re-checks current account, role, applicable assignment, School/organization scope, evidence category, record/evidence state, P0-04 authorization, and link expiry/revocation. Possession is never authority. Membership suspension/removal, access-removing role or scope change, Auditor replacement/revocation/completion, stale/replaced/restricted/unavailable evidence or report, approved deletion of the underlying record, or explicit authorized operational revocation makes the link unusable at the next retrieval check without waiting for expiry.

After the 24-hour window, the generated export copy is securely deleted without deleting canonical evidence/records, immutable history, or export-attempt/audit history. If deletion fails, access remains denied, deletion is retried by the approved operational mechanism, and failure/retry/outcome are audited. D04 is prospective and does not approve an export category under D11.

Physical cash-count/custody proof, bank statements, government-system records, original appointment/designation documents, and any physical original or externally governed record are `EXTERNAL-CONTROLLED` reference-only by default and must not enter application object storage. Policy sources do not use ordinary evidence upload; they use `SOURCE-REPOSITORY` or the separately controlled policy-source handling governed by P0-06. Reference-only evidence remains substantive evidence and retains the structured reference/verification metadata defined above.

## Privacy Classification

| Class | Meaning | Minimum handling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `PUBLIC` | Official source already lawfully public and approved for public release | Integrity and source attribution still required; public status does not make internal annotations public |
| `INTERNAL` | Non-public operational metadata with no sensitive evidence content | Authenticated access within the exact authorized organizational/command boundary |
| `CONFIDENTIAL` | School financial, commercial, report, policy-publication, or governance evidence | Private storage, least privilege, School/assigned scope, audited access/export, redaction where an aggregate does not require detail |
| `RESTRICTED` | Personal identity/contact data, credentials/authentication data, bank/account/payee data, signatures, appointment evidence, workpapers/findings, or other content whose disclosure creates heightened privacy, security, or audit risk | Named-role and exact-record authorization, re-authentication for sensitive export, no public links, no System Log content, redaction/minimization, separately recorded access/export outcome |

Classification is content-driven. A category marked `CONFIDENTIAL` becomes `RESTRICTED` when an artifact contains a personal identifier, signature, bank/account detail, credential/authentication data, protected finding, or similarly sensitive field.

Under `PO-REP-2026-08-12-D12`, effective 2026-08-13, this four-class scheme is the approved initial-pilot privacy-control baseline for existing and new records. When multiple classes apply, the most restrictive governs. No additional class is created. This retrospective operational handling rule does not create retrospective export or deletion authority and does not change D11's prospective-only scope.

### D12 Required Privacy Processes

- **Privacy notice:** required, covering information categories, operational purpose, organization/governance boundary, authorized-access principle, applicable preservation principle, and controlled-export principle without invented statutory wording or legal claims.
- **General consent workflow:** not required. Do not add a mandatory general-consent checkbox/workflow unless a later authoritative source requires consent for a specific activity.
- **Data-access request:** required as a controlled operational process, not a self-service portal. Identify requester/subject, verify identity as appropriate, determine scope, check P0-04 and classification, exclude/redact third-party or Restricted content where required, provide only the authorized response, and audit request/decision/response/outcome. It never grants database access or overrides P0-04.
- **Data correction:** required through immutable correction/revision. Identify the record, preserve prior history, record request/reason, use the existing authorized correction process, create the revision/event, and preserve audit history. D12 creates no role.
- **Restriction/hold:** required fail-closed for an applicable investigation, audit, dispute/appeal, correction/reconciliation, security/authorization incident, legal hold, or formally authorized records/privacy hold. No destructive disposal or out-of-scope export is permitted; D05-D10 rules and existing hold authority remain unchanged.
- **Privacy/security incident escalation:** required. Record the suspected incident, preserve relevant evidence/logs, restrict or suspend affected access where authorized, escalate to the designated accountable operational/governance authority, and record decision/outcome. No statutory notification period, reporting deadline, or external-notification requirement is asserted.

Minimum-necessary handling applies to collection, storage, access, and export. A Restricted field not required for the approved purpose/command is not exposed. The initial pilot does not require a self-service privacy portal, automated consent-management system, automatic anonymization engine, automatic privacy-driven deletion, or separate privacy role. D12 establishes no retention period or disposal authority; D05-D10 remain unchanged and D13 remains required.

### D11 Fixed Constraints

- P0-04 remains the authoritative authorization boundary. An approved category does not create a role, command, read permission, organization/School scope, or `AUTH-24` access.
- D04 governs every generated business-export copy and download: 24-hour expiry, immediate authorization/state re-check on retrieval, secure deletion after expiry, and fail-closed audited retry on deletion failure. No category bypasses D04.
- D10 raw backup export/restore is controlled infrastructure activity outside D11. D11 cannot enable, constrain, or substitute for that operation.
- D08 prohibits raw/bulk Audit Log export. D09 prohibits raw EV-02/EV-16 export and sensitive payload logging. Those boundaries remain unchanged.
- External-controlled originals are not exportable merely because their structured reference metadata is visible; only explicitly authorized metadata may be included.
- Requests, generation, retrieval, expiry, revocation, deletion, deletion failure/retry, and outcome events must be audited. System Logs contain only the allowlisted operational fields above and never sensitive evidence content.
- Each category decision must include a durable decision reference, effective date, and retrospective applicability. Until all fields are supplied, the category remains disabled.

## Prohibited Exports

The pilot prohibits:

1. Any cross-School financial, membership, assessment, finding, report, or evidence export not explicitly authorized by one approved category and requester scope.
2. ESAO Reviewer, cross-School comparison, ranking, or aggregate-summary export while `AUTH-24` remains `DEFERRED-AND-DENIED`.
3. Raw or bulk Audit Log export, database dumps, backups, object-store bucket/archive export, or unrestricted evidence archive.
4. Credentials, password hashes, tokens, session cookies, recovery secrets, encryption keys, full authentication request/response bodies, or security answers in any export.
5. System Logs containing or exporting evidence bodies, financial row data, personal names/emails, signatures, bank/account data, purposes, findings, report rows, or object contents.
6. Unredacted personal identifiers in ESAO aggregates or reports unless a later approved category explicitly requires and authorizes each field.
7. Draft, incomplete, stale, superseded, or replaced report export without an explicit state label and category rule permitting that state.
8. External controlled artifacts, bank statements, appointment signatures, raw audit samples, or workpaper attachments merely because their metadata/reference is viewable.
9. Export through a public/permanent URL, email attachment, notification payload, support ticket, or diagnostic bundle outside the approved controlled delivery path.
10. Export by System Admin, ESAO Admin, Policy Publisher, SESAO Auditor, School Admin, Finance Officer, or School Director outside the exact P0-04 command/data boundary; role name alone never authorizes export.

## System Log Content Contract

System Logs use an allowlist rather than attempting to redact arbitrary payloads after collection.

Allowed fields are timestamp, environment/service/component, severity, stable error/event code, request/job correlation ID, failed-export display code, route/template name without identifiers, duration, retry count, software/schema version, opaque actor/organization/target IDs when essential and separately approved, and success/failure state.

For EV-02 only, IP address and device-related metadata may be recorded when needed for authentication/security operations, investigation, fraud/abuse detection, or attributable security history. Omit it when unnecessary. D09 does not approve device fingerprinting, behavioral tracking, or additional analytics collection.

Prohibited fields are evidence/document/report content, file bytes or extracted text, financial amounts/balances/purposes, names, emails, national or employee identifiers, signatures, bank/account/payee data, audit observations/findings/responses, policy source bodies, appointment bodies, passwords/hashes/tokens/cookies/secrets/keys, full request or response bodies, SQL parameter values, and object-storage URLs or keys containing business data.

Unexpected sensitive input is recorded only as a stable validation/error code and correlation ID. Operational investigation follows the correlation ID into the separately authorized canonical evidence or Audit Log view; content is never copied into the System Log.

## D13 Disposal Control

Under `PO-REP-2026-08-12-D13`, destructive disposal is permitted only when an authoritative records/privacy policy establishes eligibility and every applicable hold is cleared. Passage of time alone never creates eligibility. Private Business / Product Owner approval is required for each disposal run; the technical executor cannot self-authorize. System Admin may execute only within an existing authorization/operational boundary, and D13 creates no role or P0-04 authority.

Disposal is prohibited while any legal hold, investigation, audit, dispute/appeal, unresolved correction/reconciliation, open obligation/Advance, replacement/stale dependency, security/authorization incident, or formally authorized records/privacy hold remains active. Each run requires a durable disposal reference, identified approver, exact record/evidence scope, eligibility basis, hold-clearance confirmation, execution time, and technical executor.

The deletion manifest records disposal-run ID, record/evidence identifiers, classification, storage location/reference, eligibility basis, approver, executor, timestamp, result, applicable hash/reference, and failure/retry outcome without sensitive evidence content. Successful deletions remain deleted. Failed targets remain protected and inaccessible under existing authorization. A retry references the original run and is audited; it does not silently create a new decision. Full success cannot be reported until every intended eligible target is verified.

Canonical deletion does not automatically destroy backup generations. Backup disposition remains governed by D10 and a later authoritative retention/disposal policy; restoration cannot circumvent canonical disposal. After execution, verify application inaccessibility, canonical reference/history consistency, preservation of immutable Audit Log history, and recorded deletion results/failures. Audit the request, approval, eligibility/hold-clearance decision, execution, each failure/retry, final verification, and outcome without deleted evidence content.

D13 enables no automatic age-based deletion, invents no retention duration, claims no statutory destruction requirement, and leaves D05-D12 unchanged. Its effective date is 2026-08-13 and it applies to evidence already held.
