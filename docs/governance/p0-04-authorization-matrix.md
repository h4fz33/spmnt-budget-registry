This is the canonical command-level authorization matrix for P0-04. Generic role names grant no authority beyond the commands explicitly listed here. `N/A` means the control is intentionally not required. `OPEN` means the value is unsupported and the command must fail closed.

The former School Director-only model is historical/superseded effective 2026-08-15 by Product Owner Decision 12. Each School still has zero or one active School Director. Acting and Temporary Substitute Director Authority are separate, named, non-delegated appointment records; neither creates an additional active School Director or a generic fallback. An Acting record identifies both the unavailable active Director and a distinct named Acting substitute. A Temporary record identifies its named Temporary substitute and the documented basis that Active/Acting resolution cannot supply a holder, including a School with zero active Director.

## Initial Authorization Bootstrap

- During the sealed one-time Initial Authorization Bootstrap, System Admin may configure any number of initial SESAO Auditor accounts using a unique authenticated application identity, person name, the SESAO Auditor role, and the SESAO Narathiwat organizational scope.
- Application identity binding is a technical requirement, not organizational appointment evidence. Bootstrap requires no appointment-document upload, evidence hash, external verification, or second-person in-application approval.
- Bootstrap configuration grants no School or Audit Assessment access by itself. An Auditor gains Assessment access only through the initial assignment created atomically by `AUTH-26` or a later ESAO Admin assignment under `AUTH-25`.
- The immutable bootstrap configuration version and audit event record the configured person, identity, role, scope, status, System Admin actor, and timestamp. After successful activation, bootstrap is permanently sealed and post-bootstrap changes follow the ordinary Matrix.

## Global Rules

- Every runtime action uses a named authenticated principal; shared accounts are prohibited.
- The initial pilot enables no generic delegation. Delegated variants remain denied. Decision 12's ESAO Admin-managed Acting/Temporary appointments are an enumerated authority model, not delegation, and are limited to `AUTH-14`/`AUTH-15`.
- No generic ESAO, platform, emergency, super-admin, or override command exists.
- Effective Director Authority resolves per School, command, and time: a valid active School Director when present and able; otherwise one valid Acting Substitute Director Authority with a distinct named substitute and documented active-Director inability; otherwise one valid Temporary Substitute Director Authority with documented basis that Active/Acting resolution cannot supply a holder, including zero active Director; otherwise deny. A resuming active Director immediately makes Acting authority ineffective. Active/Acting/Temporary conflicts, same-tier duplicate/overlap, stale/incomplete/ambiguous evidence, invalid scope, and any result with more than one effective holder fail closed. The original command SoD, evidence, authentication, and reviewer controls still apply.
- Only a valid named Acting/Temporary subject whose `AUTH-14` appointment explicitly scopes the command may resolve under `AUTH-15`, and only for `AUTH-09`, `AUTH-11`, `AUTH-12`, `AUTH-18`, or the approval portion of `AUTH-21`. `AUTH-19`, `AUTH-34`, `AUTH-35`, `AUTH-38`, all membership/audit/policy/platform commands, and every unlisted command remain active-School-Director-only or otherwise deny as their row requires.
- Historical authorization and audit evidence remains attributable after later revocation or replacement.
- System Admin is a technical executor only where a row explicitly says so.
- Any number of SESAO Auditor accounts may be configured. An authenticated configured Auditor whose account status is active may execute `AUTH-26`, which atomically creates the Assessment and its initial active assignment. Each Audit Assessment then has at most one active Auditor, and only that assigned Auditor may execute `AUTH-27`, `AUTH-31`, `AUTH-32`, or `AUTH-33` for the exact Assessment, School, audit period, and resolved checklist/policy versions.
- Auditor assignment replacement is atomic: the previous assignment ends before the replacement becomes active, no concurrent active Auditor is permitted, and completion ends executable Auditor authority for that Assessment.
- The five Auditor commands form one end-to-end workflow and require no additional Auditor participant, specialized Auditor role, or extra Auditor approval, verification, or review step.
- Each School SAR has exactly two distinct assigned assessors: assessor slot 1 is an active Finance Officer and assessor slot 2 is an authenticated individual holding only the exact School/SAR-scoped External Assessor capability created by `AUTH-35`. The active School Director cannot occupy either slot.
- SAR assessor assignments are standing assignments with immutable effective history. Finance Officer assignment invalidates on loss of the Finance Officer role, School transfer, or explicit revocation/replacement. External Assessor assignment invalidates on explicit revocation/replacement or loss of authenticated-account eligibility.
- SAR approval and submission are separate: the active School Director performs `AUTH-38` approval/signature only, and the assigned Finance Officer performs `AUTH-39` submission only after both assessor signatures, Director approval, resolved scoring, and no blocking evidence gap.
- ESAO Reviewer access under `AUTH-24` is limited to submitted SARs and permitted aggregate/report outputs. It grants no School SAR approval, signature, submission, return, mutation, assignment, ranking, or score-override authority.
