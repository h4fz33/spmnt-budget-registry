# P0-04 Final Authorization Matrix

**Status:** Product Owner-approved final authorization model; BLK-011 hybrid Substitute School Director Authority and P0-10 School Admin onboarding reconciled
**Decision authority:** Private Business / Product Owner
**Decision record:** [P0-04 — BLK-003 Resolution Record](../research/P0-04%20%E2%80%94%20BLK-003%20Resolution%20Record.md)
**Onboarding decision:** [P0-10 Registration and Onboarding Policy](p0-10-registration-onboarding-policy.md)
**Reconciled:** 2026-08-17

This is the canonical command-level authorization matrix for P0-04. Generic role names grant no authority beyond the commands explicitly listed here. `N/A` means the control is intentionally not required. `OPEN` means the value is unsupported and the command must fail closed.

The former School Director-only model is historical/superseded effective 2026-08-15 by Product Owner Decision 12 and amended by the Product Owner-approved BLK-011 hybrid model on 2026-08-16/17. Each School still has zero or one active School Director. Acting and Temporary Substitute Director Authority are separate, named, non-delegated authority records; neither creates an additional active School Director, membership, role transfer, or generic fallback. Active Director Availability is separately audited. Acting identifies the unavailable active Director and a distinct eligible same-School substitute; Temporary identifies an eligible same-School substitute and the basis that Active/Acting resolution cannot supply a holder, including a School with zero active Director.

The Product Owner-approved P0-10 amendment effective 2026-08-17 supersedes the public-registration actor semantics for the synthetic pilot. There is no public Finance Officer registration. An active School Admin may submit only a same-School synthetic Finance Officer account request; ESAO Admin alone verifies and decides it under `AUTH-03`. System Admin may execute only the exact approved technical/bootstrap record and cannot choose identity, School, membership, role, or organization authority. ADR-0003 remains historical architecture evidence; the live private-pilot onboarding contract is ADR-0017 and the P0-10 policy record.

## Initial Authorization Bootstrap

- During the sealed one-time Initial Authorization Bootstrap, System Admin may configure any number of initial SESAO Auditor accounts using a unique authenticated application identity, person name, the SESAO Auditor role, and the SESAO Narathiwat organizational scope.
- The initial School Admin bootstrap is a separate one-time path: an active ESAO Admin approves an exact synthetic manifest containing exactly one School Admin account for each of the 17 active Schools. System Admin may technically apply that manifest only after fresh re-authentication and exact integrity/replay validation. The manifest contains no password, activation secret, Finance Officer, Director, Acting/Temporary, or other role data; missing, extra, duplicate, altered, unknown, or real-person rows fail closed. Approval and technical execution are separate immutable audit events.
- Application identity binding is a technical requirement, not organizational appointment evidence. Bootstrap requires no appointment-document upload, evidence hash, external verification, or second-person in-application approval.
- Bootstrap configuration grants no School or Audit Assessment access by itself. An Auditor gains Assessment access only through the initial assignment created atomically by `AUTH-26` or a later ESAO Admin assignment under `AUTH-25`.
- The immutable bootstrap configuration version and audit event record the configured person, identity, role, scope, status, System Admin actor, and timestamp. After successful activation, bootstrap is permanently sealed and post-bootstrap changes follow the ordinary Matrix.

## Global Rules

- Every runtime action uses a named authenticated principal; shared accounts are prohibited.
- The initial pilot enables no generic delegation. Delegated variants remain denied. The stable `AUTH-14` command family has the enumerated `DIRECTOR`, `ESAO`, and `TEMP` variants; these authority records are not delegation.
- No generic ESAO, platform, emergency, super-admin, or override command exists.
- Effective Director Authority resolves per School, command, and time: a valid active School Director when present and able; otherwise one valid `IN_FORCE` Acting authority; otherwise one valid `IN_FORCE` Temporary authority; otherwise deny. Active assignment and cross-tier records may coexist only when precedence resolves exactly one holder. Same-tier duplicates, contradictory availability/scope/lifecycle state, stale/incomplete/ambiguous evidence, invalid scope/status, or more than one effective holder fail closed.
- Active Director Availability is created atomically by `AUTH-14/DIRECTOR`, `AUTH-14/ESAO`, or, when an active Director exists and no Acting holder is available, `AUTH-14/TEMP`. Unavailability persists independently of substitute expiry/revocation/supersession and ends only through an explicit authenticated return/resumption event or termination of the active-Director assignment. System Admin cannot change it.
- Only a valid named Acting/Temporary subject whose `AUTH-14` record explicitly scopes the command may resolve under `AUTH-15`, and only for `AUTH-09`, `AUTH-11`, `AUTH-12`, or `AUTH-18`. `AUTH-19`, `AUTH-21`, `AUTH-34`, `AUTH-35`, `AUTH-38`, all membership/audit/policy/platform commands, and every unlisted command remain active-School-Director-only or otherwise deny as their row requires.
- All underlying command evidence, authentication, reviewer, and person-level SoD controls remain binding. A substitute cannot approve their own prepared payment, prepare/verify/approve the same Daily Balance, or assign themselves as Daily Balance Verifier; additive Finance Officer/School Admin roles do not relax SoD.
- Historical authorization and audit evidence remains attributable after later revocation or replacement.
- System Admin is a technical executor only where a row explicitly says so.
- Any number of SESAO Auditor accounts may be configured. An authenticated configured Auditor whose account status is active may execute `AUTH-26`, which atomically creates the Assessment and its initial active assignment. Each Audit Assessment then has at most one active Auditor, and only that assigned Auditor may execute `AUTH-27`, `AUTH-31`, `AUTH-32`, or `AUTH-33` for the exact Assessment, School, audit period, and resolved checklist/policy versions.
- Auditor assignment replacement is atomic: the previous assignment ends before the replacement becomes active, no concurrent active Auditor is permitted, and completion ends executable Auditor authority for that Assessment.
- The five Auditor commands form one end-to-end workflow and require no additional Auditor participant, specialized Auditor role, or extra Auditor approval, verification, or review step.
- Each School SAR has exactly two distinct assigned assessors: assessor slot 1 is an active Finance Officer and assessor slot 2 is an authenticated individual holding only the exact School/SAR-scoped External Assessor capability created by `AUTH-35`. The active School Director cannot occupy either slot.
- SAR assessor assignments are standing assignments with immutable effective history. Finance Officer assignment invalidates on loss of the Finance Officer role, School transfer, or explicit revocation/replacement. External Assessor assignment invalidates on explicit revocation/replacement or loss of authenticated-account eligibility.
- SAR approval and submission are separate: the active School Director performs `AUTH-38` approval/signature only, and the assigned Finance Officer performs `AUTH-39` submission only after both assessor signatures, Director approval, resolved scoring, and no blocking evidence gap.
- ESAO Reviewer access under `AUTH-24` is limited to submitted SARs and permitted aggregate/report outputs. It grants no School SAR approval, signature, submission, return, mutation, assignment, ranking, or score-override authority.
