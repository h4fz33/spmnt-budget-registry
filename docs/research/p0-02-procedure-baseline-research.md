# P0-02 Procedure-Baseline Research

**Status:** completed

**Research date:** 2026-08-08

**Pilot authority:** [SESAO Narathiwat Pilot Governance Charter](../governance/pilot-charter.md)

## Purpose And Result

P0-02 requires one unambiguous, authoritative procedure baseline for each School. The supplied OBEC guide and the SESAO Narathiwat current-applicability confirmation now meet that acceptance condition for the complete actual school-user population.

All 17 Schools are the project's complete actual school-user population, not an experimental subset, and are in the approved boundary of the Secondary Educational Service Area Office Narathiwat (SESAO Narathiwat). The supplied OBEC assessment guide is an authoritative OBEC Internal Audit Group assessment instrument. It covers secondary-education-area schools (and some primary schools) that follow the B.E. 2515 manual, while recognizing the B.E. 2544 subunit-control system as another procedure family. On 2026-08-08, SESAO Narathiwat confirmed that every School in that population follows the policy, regulations, and forms in the B.E. 2515 guide. Together with the approved 17-school SESAO boundary, this selects the B.E. 2515 baseline for every School.

The original B.E. 2515 issuance's publication/commencement record is not held in the repository. This is an archival provenance gap, not a current baseline ambiguity: the source revision is B.E. 2515, and its effective applicability for SchoolBanchee is confirmed for the entire 17-School actual school-user population from 2026-08-08 until superseded by a SESAO-activated Policy Version sourced from OBEC.

## Source Comparison

### Source Facts

- `S1` and `S5` identify the B.E. 2515 manual as a recognized procedure for secondary-education-area schools, while `S5` also defines the alternative B.E. 2544 system.
- `S8`, combined with the charter's 17-School actual school-user population under SESAO Narathiwat, selects B.E. 2515 for every School. No school-specific exception was declared.
- `S1` describes the 2515 manual as the Ministry of Finance subunit accounting procedure, with control registers and accounting records; it also identifies daily inspection, receipt-book control, advance controls, daily balance reports, monthly reporting, annual school-revenue reporting, and annual self-assessment as relevant control areas.
- `S2`/`S6` is an authenticated Royal Gazette copy of the Ministry of Finance B.E. 2562 regulation. Its clause 2 commencement is the day after publication: 14 May 2562 (2019-05-14), and clause 3 identifies the repealed predecessor regulations. The source is an overlay for government withdrawal, receipt, payment, custody, and remittance; it does not select the subunit baseline.
- `S3` describes the B.E. 2544 control system as a simplified arrangement for small primary-school subunits. `S5` confirms that OBEC recognizes both B.E. 2515 and B.E. 2544 in its assessment definitions; `S8` selects B.E. 2515 rather than B.E. 2544 for the pilot.
- `S7` is an official EEF B.E. 2564 regulation. It is effective 9 November 2564 after its 8 November 2564 Royal Gazette publication and repeals the EEF B.E. 2561 regulation, but no supplied evidence establishes an EEF fund flow at any pilot School.

### Inference

- **School mapping:** the approved charter establishes the complete 17-School actual school-user population under SESAO Narathiwat, and `S8` applies the B.E. 2515 guide to that common population. Therefore, every matrix row maps to the same B.E. 2515 baseline.
- **Excluded alternative:** B.E. 2544 remains recognized background material but is not a pilot baseline.
- **Form applicability:** the SESAO confirmation includes the guide's forms as part of the current pilot baseline. P0-05 still determines which anonymized/signed examples are needed for validation.

### Residual Non-Blocking Gaps

1. The original B.E. 2515 manual's publication/commencement record, amendment history, and document number are not stored in the repository. The current pilot baseline is nevertheless unambiguous through `S5` plus `S8`; retain this as archival provenance work and revisit it before any future baseline replacement.
2. The B.E. 2562 Ministry of Finance overlay is effective from 14 May 2562. P0-03 must enumerate the exact flow-level controls and exceptions; it does not reopen the B.E. 2515 baseline selection.
3. No EEF fund flow is evidenced for any pilot School. `S7` is explicitly excluded from the universal baseline and must be resolved only if an EEF flow enters the pilot scope.
4. A future change must be supported by an OBEC-issued source and activated by an authorized SESAO Policy Publisher with required source and audit evidence; it may not silently replace the B.E. 2515 baseline. Pre-activation second-person review is not required, while P0-04 requires Order 452/2568 current-status verification, approved Policy Publisher designation, and separately approved publication scope.

## Baseline Resolution

This resolution applies to the project’s complete actual school-user population: all 17 Schools under SESAO Narathiwat. SESAO Narathiwat is that population's audit/oversight unit, and each School remains its own financial-record boundary.

All 17 pilot Schools use `คู่มือการบัญชีสำหรับหน่วยงานย่อย พ.ศ. 2515` and the policy, regulations, and forms in the supplied OBEC assessment guide. The common current pilot applicability begins on 2026-08-08 and remains in force until an appointed SESAO Policy Publisher activates a superseding Policy Version sourced from OBEC. The Ministry of Finance B.E. 2562 regulation is the verified general overlay, effective 14 May 2562. No B.E. 2544 or EEF exception is in the current universal baseline.
