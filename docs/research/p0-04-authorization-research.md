## Scope

This is the live architectural authorization and segregation-of-duties matrix. It applies the nine final BLK-003 decisions at the role/capability level and does not invent personnel names, appointments, reviewers, approval authorities, or verifier capabilities.

Runtime actions must be performed by an authenticated, attributable principal. Policy Publisher eligibility is established by current official Internal Audit position/assignment evidence. The approved Authorization Matrix designates exactly one current holder and one standby alternate. P0-04 cannot close until Order 452/2568 current-status verification, the separately approved application publication scope, and final matrix approval are recorded.

`OPEN` means the required value has not been established by an authoritative source. An Audit Log is evidence, not an approver or reviewer.

## Pilot Scope Rule

Any privileged command whose required actor, scope, approver, reviewer, authentication, delegation, segregation-of-duties, or audit value remains `OPEN` is a **Deferred Privileged Command**: it is excluded from the current pilot and must be denied. It may enter scope only through a later approved Policy Version or P0-04 matrix decision. This rule narrows the pilot authorization surface; it does not weaken any of the nine final BLK-003 decisions or the mandatory segregation controls.

The pilot enables **no delegation initially**. Delegation remains permitted by the architectural BLK-003 decision, but routine-posting, payment/advance approval, monthly-close, inspection/verification, and other delegated variants are Deferred Privileged Commands until delegation authority, scope, revocation, invalidation, and expiry are approved.

The pilot also excludes **Temporary Director Approval** initially. When no authenticated School Director is active, every Director-required command is denied. The alternate mechanism approved by BLK-003 remains available for a later matrix decision, but its issuer, command scope, duration, reviewer, evidence, and segregation-of-duties restrictions are not inferred for the pilot.

The pilot must still support a **complete auditable month**. Therefore `VerifyDailyBalance`, `ApproveDailyBalance`, reconciliation acceptance, and `CloseMonth` remain required in pilot scope and are gating decisions; they are not deferred merely because their verifier/acceptance details are currently `OPEN`.

## Delegation Rules

The existing School Director delegation mechanism may cover payment approval, advance approval, monthly close, inspection/verification, and routine posting. Every delegation must use a compatible existing organizational role, the same School/organizational scope, an explicit capability, no self-approval, no re-delegation, explicit revocation, and immediate invalidation when the delegator loses the underlying authority.

Routine-posting delegation grants posting only; it grants no approval authority. Policy Publisher, ESAO Admin, System Admin, and organization membership administration cannot be delegated through this mechanism. Temporary Director Approval is a separate constrained alternate mechanism, not a delegation shortcut.

## Decision Effects

1. **Policy Publisher organizational evidence and designation:** Order 452/2568 establishes eligibility from current official Internal Audit position/assignment evidence. The approved Authorization Matrix designates exactly one current holder, `นางบังอร วันริโก`, and one standby alternate, `นายอานุงรุสลัน ดาโวะ`. No separate Policy Publisher Appointment Memorandum or separate SESAO Auditor appointment document is required. System Admin is only the technical executor; replacement requires updated authoritative evidence, current-status verification, and an updated approved designation. Application publication scope is separately approved and is not attributed to Order 452/2568.
2. **Administration boundary:** System Admin owns platform account/registration lifecycle only. Every formally appointed ESAO Admin owns organization membership administration, School assignment, and School-level role assignment across all 17 SESAO Narathiwat pilot Schools, with no per-admin School subset, financial authority, privileged-capability assignment, self-administration, or delegation in the initial pilot.
   The ESAO Admin is the sole membership decision maker with no second reviewer; state-changing decisions require fresh re-authentication, structured reasons where applicable, immediate authorization-version invalidation, and complete Audit Log evidence.
   ESAO Admin also assigns, replaces, or revokes exactly one active School Director per School from formal external appointment evidence; replacement is atomic, audited, and invalidates both old and new authorization revisions.
   SESAO Product Owner/accountable reviewer externally appoints or revokes ESAO Admin. System Admin may technically apply only the exact evidence-backed record with fresh re-authentication and no person/scope discretion.
   The same external-authority/technical-application split applies to SESAO Auditor appointment/revocation; organization-wide eligibility grants no School evidence access until a Cycle assignment exists.
3. **Temporary Director Approval:** The constrained alternate remains an approved future mechanism but is excluded and denied in the initial pilot. Without an active School Director, every Director-required command is denied; later activation requires a new approved matrix decision for all detailed values.
4. **Receipt Book:** Finance Officer issuance needs no mandatory second-person authorization. The former "approval" field is custody/issuance evidence.
5. **Routine posting:** It is within the existing delegation mechanism and remains subject to all listed safeguards; it grants no approval authority.
6. **Privileged Correction:** Authenticated School Director Approval is mandatory before execution. Proposer/preparer self-approval is prohibited; no separate independent pre-execution reviewer is required in the pilot, and immutable evidence is retained for later approved oversight access.
7. **Daily Balance:** Finance Officer preparation, verification by a different authenticated School Admin or Finance Officer holding the School-scoped `Daily Balance Verifier` capability, then authenticated School Director approval/signature are ordered controls. The authenticated School Director for the same School assigns or prospectively revokes the capability with strong re-authentication and no second approver/reviewer, cannot assign themselves, and cannot erase historical verification evidence. The verifier cannot prepare, approve/sign, solely reconcile, or close the same report and cannot delegate the capability in the initial pilot.
8. **Policy lifecycle:** A newer activation supersedes the prior version. Historical resolution remains preserved; there is no retirement/deactivation command.
9. **ESAO Reviewer:** The capability remains read/review/compare/report only but is excluded and denied in the initial pilot. Any later assigned-School or aggregate-reporting scope requires a new approved matrix decision.
10. **Monthly Reconciliation and Close:** Finance Officer prepares; School Director accepts and closes with strong re-authentication; no delegation or separate reviewer is enabled in the pilot; unresolved discrepancies block acceptance and close; the preparer cannot accept or close.
11. **School Financial Accounting Audit Assessment:** Any appointed SESAO Auditor may create one School/period Cycle and assign one working and one different finalizing holder of the same role; the creator may occupy either position, never both. Assignment/reassignment/finalization require fresh re-authentication, access is Cycle/School-scoped, revocation blocks until replacement, and neither holder may mutate School canonical records. ESAO Reviewer access, generic aggregate access, ranking, and aggregate summaries remain excluded and denied.
12. **Generic privileged administration:** No generic privileged role/capability grant, change, or revocation command exists in the initial pilot. Only separately enumerated evidence-backed appointment commands may be enabled; every unlisted capability is deferred and denied.
