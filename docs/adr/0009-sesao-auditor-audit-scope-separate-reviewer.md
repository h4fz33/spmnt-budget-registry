# SESAO Auditor Audit Scope Is Separate From ESAO Reviewer

**Status:** superseded by ADR-0012

The initial pilot grants appointed SESAO Auditors audit-assessment permissions within explicitly assigned Audit Assessment Cycles; it does not assign the separate ESAO Reviewer role. One SESAO Auditor performs and submits the assigned cycle, while a different holder of the same SESAO Auditor role independently verifies findings and finalizes/accepts it. ESAO Reviewer access, cross-school comparison, ranking, and aggregate reporting remain excluded and denied; Policy Publisher remains a separate capability.
