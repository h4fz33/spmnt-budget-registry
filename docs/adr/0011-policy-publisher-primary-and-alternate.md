# Policy Publisher Primary And Alternate

**Status:** accepted

SESAO Product Owner/accountable reviewer externally designates one active Policy Publisher and one named standby alternate from active SESAO Auditors. System Admin may technically apply only the exact evidence-backed record; only the current holder may publish, while replacement evidence atomically activates the alternate and removes the prior holder. ADR-0006's no-second-approval, no-pre-activation-review, no-delegation, and no-fixed-expiry rules remain unchanged.
