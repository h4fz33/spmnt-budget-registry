# Policy Publisher Primary And Alternate

**Status:** accepted

Order 452/2568 establishes eligibility through current official Internal Audit position/assignment evidence for `นางบังอร วันริโก` and `นายอานุงรุสลัน ดาโวะ`. The Product Owner/accountable reviewer designates one current Policy Publisher and one named standby alternate through the approved Authorization Matrix; no separate Policy Publisher Appointment Memorandum or separate SESAO Auditor appointment document is required. System Admin may technically apply only the exact approved designation and evidence; it cannot select, originate, modify, or self-grant the designation. Only the current holder may publish, while updated evidence, current-status verification, and an updated designation atomically activate the alternate and remove the prior holder. ADR-0006's no-second-approval, no-pre-activation-review, no-delegation, and no-fixed-expiry rules remain unchanged.
