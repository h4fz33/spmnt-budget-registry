# Substitute School Director Authority

**Status:** accepted
**Date:** 2026-08-15; amended 2026-08-17

Private Business / Product Owner approved a constrained substitute-authority model for School Director-required commands. Each School retains zero or one active School Director. Only an able active Director, otherwise one valid Acting authority, otherwise one valid Temporary authority may be the Effective Director Authority for the exact School, command, and time; all other cases deny. The model is deliberately not a role, membership, generic delegation, emergency override, or additional active Director.

An Acting record identifies the unavailable active Director and a distinct same-School substitute with a fixed reason code and optional expiry. Active Director Availability is separately audited: Acting expiry, revocation, or supersession does not restore availability, and only explicit authenticated return/resumption does. A Temporary record identifies an eligible authenticated Finance Officer or School Admin of the same School, a required Active/Acting-unavailability basis, and a mandatory expiry. Subject-eligibility loss permanently invalidates either authority. Replacement and correction are atomic immutable supersessions, as is separate `AUTH-05` conversion from Temporary to active Director; failure leaves the prior authority unchanged.

The stable `AUTH-14` command family has `DIRECTOR`, `ESAO`, and `TEMP` actor-specific variants: an active Director may create Acting authority, ESAO Admin may create/manage Acting authority, and ESAO Admin alone manages Temporary authority. Cross-tier records may coexist only when precedence resolves one effective holder; same-tier duplicates, contradictory state, and multiple effective holders deny. Acting reasons use the fixed initial `MEDICAL_LEAVE`, `OFFICIAL_TRAVEL`, `PERSONAL_LEAVE`, or `OTHER` allowlist; `OTHER` requires explanation. Every immutable application authorization record has an integrity digest and an optional document has its own SHA-256 hash under the downstream `INTERNAL-AUTHORIZATION` export category. System Admin gains no lifecycle or financial authority. `AUTH-15` remains limited to `AUTH-09`, `AUTH-11`, `AUTH-12`, and `AUTH-18`, with all underlying person-level SoD binding.
